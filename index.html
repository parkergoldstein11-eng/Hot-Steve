How the power rankings work

Each team gets a score out of 100. All-play win rate counts for 35 percent, points per game 25 percent, the last two weeks of scoring 15 percent, and roster strength 25 percent. Roster strength is the FantasyCalc redraft value of each team's best healthy starting lineup. Movement arrows compare against the rankings through the previous week.

Sources
